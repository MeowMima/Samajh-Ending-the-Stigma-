Our Philosophy

We believe understanding is healing.

Samajh is not a therapy app. We don't track moods or give you scores. We believe the simple act of learning, reflecting, and exploring — with curiosity and without judgment — is one of the most powerful things you can do for your mind.

No diagnoses. No dashboards. Just knowledge, stories, and a gentle space to understand yourself a little better each day.
